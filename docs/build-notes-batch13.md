Area score = recency-weighted (0.88^k) mean of the last 12 quality points, needs 3; −10% after 14 idle days, −25% after 30. Drills: (acc − 0.4)/0.5. Bank: clean 1, hinted ½, missed 0. MM games: 0.6·(1 − quoteErr/4) + 0.4·(made money). Coding: solved / (half the bank). Firm score = weighted mean, missing areas count 0. Weights are the app's judgement. Stars in localStorage `tip-ready-stars`. Questions in the bank have no firm tags, so firm-reported questions are not weighted extra.
- Daily Challenge: 20 questions, 300 s cap, seeded by the local date (same for everyone): 7 arithmetic (80-in-8 generator), 4 sequences, 5 probability (Beat the Odds families), 4 order books, in the fixed order `PLAN`. Plays store `parts` per section; streak from play dates. Drill gained `spec.seed`, `spec.onRecord`; Setup gained `def.fixed` (hides difficulty).
- Mistake tags: every missed drill question is logged once at session end into progress `errs[]` (last 400: id, ts, src, text, tag, g = guess). Guess: timed out/skipped → time; within 5%, ×10/÷10, sign flip or same digits → slip; 1 − p on a probability → misread; else concept. The result review shows a select per miss; the dashboard shows "Mistakes by cause" for 30 days.
